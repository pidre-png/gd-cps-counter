# CPS Counter

Shows three counters while you play:

- **CPS** — your clicks per second in real time (tracked over a configurable window)
- **Max** — the highest CPS you reached (per attempt or per level)
- **Clicks** — total clicks for the current attempt or the whole level session

Everything is configurable in the mod settings: which counters to show, what counts
as a click (all buttons or jump only), text scale, position, opacity.

Built for Geometry Dash **2.2081** with **Geode v5.11.2**.
